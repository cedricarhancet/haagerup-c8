## Coordinates, normalizations and supplementary checks

- The monomial basis, root numbering and cocycle are defined in Section 10 of the paper. No script name is needed to define this lattice convention.
- The residue-map convention is implemented by find_prime and Fp in [modp.py](calculs-sources/cas-II/modp.py), which fix both square-root choices. The exact F4 map at 8388571 is stated explicitly in the proof.
- The fixed Griess basis and numbering are described by [the construction](calculs-sources/cas-II/griess_subalgebras.py) and [its output](calculs-sources/cas-II/griess_subalgebras.json).
- The ordered primary lines for subalgebra 9 are in [isotropic_lines_II_9.json](calculs-sources/cas-II/isotropic_lines_II_9.json). The case-(I), subalgebra-2 ordered eigenspaces are obtained by admissible_spaces(\'I\', 2) in [isotropic_lines.py](calculs-sources/cas-II/isotropic_lines.py).
- For the simple-current word, the seven exponential coefficients are printed in Table 4. They give the normalization directly, independently of a software function name.
- The input coordinates for the 310 independent generator products are in [generator-inputs.json](checks/independent-modes/generator-inputs.json); [the README](checks/independent-modes/README.md) gives the portable replay.
- The exact commutant vectors and Virasoro matrices are in [c4-exact-witnesses.json](checks/identification/c4/c4-exact-witnesses.json). The data order the two Virasoro vectors by increasing central charge, opposite to the indexing in Proposition 10.31.
- For Proposition 10.41, case (I), the retained script [j6_caseI_action.py](calculs-sources/jumeau/j6_caseI_action.py) labels z_1 and z_A by (-1,-1,1) and (-1,1,1), and their product by (1,-1,1).
- The complete F4 certificate is indexed by [ARCHIVES-F4.json](checks/certificates/deliverable/ARCHIVES-F4.json), with its exhaustive receipt in [COMPLETE-F4.json](checks/certificates/deliverable/COMPLETE-F4.json). Both large coordinate volumes are required.
- The Riesen defining-state input is external. The imported and transported data are retained in [r1_riesen_transported.pkl](calculs-sources/cas-I/r1_riesen_transported.pkl), with [identification results](calculs-sources/cas-I/r1_identify_riesen.json) and [location results](calculs-sources/cas-I/r2_locate_riesen.json). See the external-input note in REPRODUCIBILITY.md.
